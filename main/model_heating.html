5.9. Heat pump, electric boiler and thermal storage

5.9.1. Description

These models top up and shift geothermal heat for the 100 °C district-heating supply: a high-temperature heat pump (HTHP) that lifts district water with heat drawn from the brine, an electric boiler for peaks and back-up, and a thermal storage (seasonal HT-ATES or cold storage). The heat pump and the electric boiler are static models; the storage is a dynamic model (gemini_model.model_abstract.DynamicModel) whose state is the stored energy. They are registered for project JSON files as heatpump, electric_boiler and thermal_storage.

Every model outputs power_el (electricity used, W), power_th (useful heat delivered, W) and emission (0 kg/s; grid CO₂ is counted by the economics). Each model folder holds a REFERENCE.md with the equations, defaults and citations (reference cards RC-04, RC-09, RC-10). The defaults are recorded in config/assumptions.yaml (HP-ETA-L, HP-APPROACH, VER-HP-ETA, VER-HP-LIFT, EB-ETA, STO-REC-HT, STO-REC-COLD).

5.9.2. Heat pump: closed-form path

gemini_model.heatpump.heatpump_basic.HeatpumpBasic keeps GEMINI’s Nelder-Mead solver as the default (solver: "nelder_mead"; see Surface Equipment). With solver: "closed_form" the same operating point is found without a starting guess (Geo-Heat Center Guidebook 1989, Ch13; RC-04):

\[\bar T = \frac{T_{out} - T_{in}}{\ln(T_{out}/T_{in})}, \qquad COP = \eta_L \frac{\bar T_{sink}}{\bar T_{sink} - \bar T_{source}}, \qquad Q_h = Q_{src} \frac{COP}{COP - 1}, \qquad P_{el} = \frac{Q_h}{COP}\]

with \(\eta_L = 0.5\) (mode: "lorenz", water log-mean temperatures in K). The sink leaves at Th_out_target; the source outlet is the bracketed root of the energy balance. If it would fall below Ts_in_minimum it is held there and the sink outlet drops instead (as GEMINI). Where Nelder-Mead converges to a physical point the two solvers agree within 2 % (in practice 1e-5).

5.9.3. HTHP cap and datasheet limits

On the closed-form path the result is capped at a fraction of the ideal cycle at the refrigerant temperatures (condensing = sink outlet + 4 K, evaporating = source outlet − 4 K):

\[COP \le f \, \frac{T_{cond}}{T_{cond} - T_{evap}}\]

with \(f = 0.6\) without a datasheet (RC-04 default, the verifier’s VER-HP-ETA). For the RC-04 design case (district water 82 → 100 °C, brine 60 °C in) the uncapped Lorenz value is 4.79 and the capped one 3.71.

HTHP limits are MISSING until a datasheet is cited. The parameters datasheet (citation), max_lift_k, max_sink_temperature_degc and max_carnot_fraction default to None; limits without a citation are refused. Without a datasheet the outlet-to-outlet temperature difference is screened against 60 K (VER-HP-LIFT) and the maximum sink temperature is not checked. The outputs missing_inputs, within_limits, validity_warnings, source_heat_w and heat_pump_point (the verifier’s HeatPumpPoint evidence) are given by both solvers. dea_reference_cop interpolates the DEA performance table (40–100 K) as a plausibility band only.

5.9.4. Electric boiler

gemini_model.electric_boiler.electric_boiler.ElectricBoiler: \(P_{el} = Q_{th} / 0.99\) (spec GM-08, DEA). Inputs heat_demand_w, or water_flow_rate_m3s with water_temperature_in_degc (heated to water_temperature_out_degc, default 100 °C). The duty is capped at nominal_capacity_w and the rest reported as unmet_heat_w.

5.9.5. Thermal storage

gemini_model.thermal_storage.thermal_storage.ThermalStorage (RC-09):

\[E_{t+1} = E_t - \lambda \frac{\Delta t}{1\,d} E_t + \eta_c P_c \Delta t - \frac{P_d \Delta t}{\eta_d}\]

Inputs charge_power_w or discharge_power_w (W) and optional time_step_s (default one day). Parameters: capacity_mwh, initial_soc, power limits, storage_type (ht_ates or cold). The cycle recovery \(\eta_c \eta_d\) is put on discharge: 0.63 for HT-ATES (TU Delft) and 0.96 for cold storage (RTES 0.96–0.99). Outputs include energy_mwh, soc, losses_w, the cumulative charged and discharged energy and an energy-balance residual.

5.9.6. Example project

{
  "components": {
    "hthp": {"type": "heatpump", "parameters": {"mode": "lorenz", "solver": "closed_form",
             "Th_out_target": 100.0, "Ts_in_minimum": 10.0, "Cp_s": 4100.0, "rho_s": 1030.0}},
    "eboiler": {"type": "electric_boiler", "parameters": {"nominal_capacity_w": 5.0e6}}
  },
  "boundary_inputs": [
    {"component": "hthp", "key": "Th_in", "value": 82.0},
    {"component": "hthp", "key": "Ts_in", "value": 60.0},
    {"component": "hthp", "key": "hot_flow_rate", "value": 0.02778},
    {"component": "hthp", "key": "source_flow_rate", "value": 0.02222}
  ],
  "connections": [
    {"predecessor": "hthp", "successor": "eboiler",
     "ports": [["Th_out", "water_temperature_in_degc"],
               ["hot_flow_rate", "water_flow_rate_m3s"]]}
  ]
}

5.9.7. References

  • Geothermal Direct Use Engineering & Design Guidebook, Geo-Heat Center (1989): Ch13 heat pumps p.250 (water ΔT, fouling), p.258 (COP 4.41 at 15.6 / 48.9 °C), p.260 (peaking practice), Table 13.2 (1989 condenser limits, outdated).

  • Danish Energy Agency technology catalogues: Industrial Process Heat ch. 2 (high-temperature heat pumps, COP by lift); Electricity & District Heating ch. 41 (electric boilers, η 0.99).

  • TU Delft campus HT-ATES study (recovery ≈ 63 % after 10 years); RTES data-centre cooling paper (cold storage recovery 96–99 %, citing Pepin 2021).
