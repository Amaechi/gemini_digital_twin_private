5.8. Cooling

5.8.1. Description

The cooling models describe how district cooling (20 °C supply, 26 °C return) can be produced next to a geothermal doublet: a hot-water driven absorption chiller, an electric (vapour-compression) chiller, heat rejection to ambient (wet cooling tower or dry cooler) and free cooling (a water-side economiser). All four are static models (gemini_model.model_abstract.StaticModel) registered for project JSON files as absorption_chiller, electric_chiller, heat_rejection and free_cooling.

Units follow the GEMINI surface models: temperatures in °C, flows in m³/s, powers in W. New keys carry unit suffixes (_degc, _m3s, _w, _k). Every model outputs power_el (electricity used, W), power_th (useful cooling delivered, W; 0 for heat rejection) and emission (0 kg/s). Ambient dry-bulb and wet-bulb temperatures are inputs; the agent computes daily values from the KNMI normals (Stull wet-bulb) in gemini_agent.tools.climate.

Each model folder holds a REFERENCE.md with the equations, defaults and citations (reference cards RC-05, RC-06, RC-07). The defaults are recorded in config/assumptions.yaml (COOL-ABS-*, COOL-ELC, COOL-HR, COOL-FC).

5.8.2. Absorption chiller

gemini_model.absorption_chiller.absorption_chiller.AbsorptionChiller models a single-effect LiBr/H₂O chiller (Geo-Heat Center Guidebook 1989, Ch14 p.261–270). Two methods:

Method A (method: "ghc1989"), temperatures in °F:

\[F_T = \frac{-239 + 1.65\,T_{hw} - 0.001\,T_{hw}^2}{100}, \qquad F_c = \frac{323 - 0.838\,T_{chw} - (3.77 - 0.0358\,T_{chw})\,T_{cw}}{100}\]
\[Q_{avail} = F_T\,F_c\,Q_{nom}, \qquad COP = \frac{0.67\,(55.4 + 0.185\,T_{hw})}{100}\]

valid for 40–50 °F chilled and 75–90 °F cooling water. Outside, Method A is a lower bound: by default chilled water above 50 °F is evaluated at 50 °F and cooling water below 75 °F at 75 °F.

Method B (method: "characteristic", default), the characteristic equation:

\[\Delta\Delta t = (\bar t_G - \bar t_{AC}) - B\,(\bar t_{AC} - \bar t_E), \qquad Q_E = s_E\,(\Delta\Delta t - \Delta\Delta t_{min}), \qquad Q_G = s_G\,(\Delta\Delta t - \Delta\Delta t_{min,G})\]

with \(B = 1.15\) and coefficients calibrated to Method A at its rating points (44 °F chilled, 85 °F cooling water, 180–240 °F hot water) by gemini_model.absorption_chiller.characteristic.calibrate_to_ghc1989: \(s_E = 0.022260\) and \(s_G = 0.032491\) per K (fractions of nominal capacity), \(\Delta\Delta t_{min} = 9.503\) K, \(\Delta\Delta t_{min,G} = 7.453\) K. On the rating points it reproduces Method A within 1.0 % (capacity) and 2.1 % (COP). Results at 20 °C chilled water are extrapolated and flagged in validity_warnings. Capacity never exceeds the nameplate.

Energy balance: \(Q_{reject} = Q_E + Q_G + P_{aux}\) with \(P_{aux}\) = 0.077 kW/ton (≈ 2.2 % of \(Q_E\), extra tower fan and pump) while include_heat_rejection_auxiliaries is true (default); set it to false when a heat_rejection component is connected, which counts the fan and pumps itself (\(P_{aux} = 0\)). Each external circuit (drive, cooling water, chilled water) has its own density and heat capacity; with a given flow its outlet temperature follows from \(\rho c_p V \Delta T = Q\), otherwise a fixed ΔT is used (generator 7.2 K, cooling-water range 5 K) and the required flow is reported. With a given drive flow, Method B needs \(\rho c_p V_G \ge s_G/2\) (output minimum_drive_flow_rate_m3s; otherwise the drive outlet would fall below the minimum generator temperature and the machine is treated as off), and Method A keeps the drive outlet at or above its 71.4 °C zero-capacity temperature. At part load Method B lowers \(\Delta\Delta t\), so the COP falls at low load.

Input

Description

drive_temperature_in_degc

Hot-water (brine or clean loop) inlet temperature (°C)

cooling_water_temperature_in_degc

Cooling-water inlet temperature (°C), e.g. a heat-rejection unit’s water_temperature_out_degc

cooling_load_w or chilled_water_flow_rate_m3s

Cooling demand (W), or chilled-water flow (m³/s) with load \(\rho c_p V (T_{return} - T_{supply})\)

5.8.3. Electric chiller

gemini_model.electric_chiller.electric_chiller.ElectricChiller (Guidebook p.257–258):

\[COP = \eta\,\frac{T_{evap}}{T_{cond} - T_{evap}}, \qquad P_{el} = \frac{Q}{COP}, \qquad Q_{reject} = Q + P_{el}\]

with \(T_{evap} = T_{supply} - 4\) K, \(T_{cond} = T_{wb} + 4.5 + 5 + 3\) (cooling tower) or \(T_{db} + 8\) (dry cooler), COP between 3 and 8. Inputs: cooling_load_w or chilled_water_flow_rate_m3s; ambient_wet_bulb_degc or ambient_dry_bulb_degc; optional cooling_water_temperature_in_degc (then \(T_{cond} = T_{cw,in} + 5 + 3\)). η is on this refrigerant basis: calibrated so that the Guidebook point (COP 4.39 at 7.2 °C chilled / 32.2 °C condenser leaving water) is reproduced, \(\eta = 4.39 \times 32 / 276.35 = 0.508\) (the Guidebook’s 0.39–0.40 is on the water basis; RC-06). With this η the COP cap of 8 binds at the Dutch design points. \(P_{el}\) is compressor power only.

5.8.4. Heat rejection

gemini_model.heat_rejection.heat_rejection.HeatRejection (technology: cooling_tower or dry_cooler): leaving water \(T_{wb} + 4.5\) K (tower) or \(T_{db} + 5\) K (dry cooler), range 5 K, fan + pump power 2 % (tower) or 6 % (dry cooler) of heat_load_w, tower evaporation \(Q / h_{fg}\). chiller_heat_rejection_w gives \(Q_{reject} = Q_{cool}(1 + 1/COP)\).

5.8.5. Free cooling

gemini_model.free_cooling.free_cooling.FreeCooling: the economiser delivers water at \(T_{fc} = T_{wb} + 4.5 + 2\) K. Full free cooling when \(T_{fc} \le 20\) °C (\(T_{wb} \le 13.5\) °C); partial pre-cooling of the 26 °C return while \(T_{fc} < 26\) °C (fraction \((26 - T_{fc})/6\), the rest goes to a chiller via residual_cooling_w and chilled_water_temperature_out_degc); power 2 % of the heat moved.

5.8.6. Example project

{
  "components": {
    "economiser": {"type": "free_cooling", "parameters": {}},
    "chiller": {"type": "electric_chiller", "parameters": {"nominal_capacity_w": 5e6}},
    "tower": {"type": "heat_rejection", "parameters": {}}
  },
  "boundary_inputs": [
    {"component": "economiser", "key": "ambient_wet_bulb_degc", "value": 15.4},
    {"component": "economiser", "key": "cooling_load_w", "value": 4e6},
    {"component": "chiller", "key": "ambient_wet_bulb_degc", "value": 15.4},
    {"component": "tower", "key": "ambient_wet_bulb_degc", "value": 15.4}
  ],
  "connections": [
    {"predecessor": "economiser", "successor": "chiller",
     "ports": [["residual_cooling_w", "cooling_load_w"],
               ["chilled_water_temperature_out_degc", "chilled_water_temperature_in_degc"]]},
    {"predecessor": "chiller", "successor": "tower",
     "predecessor_output": "heat_rejected_w", "successor_input": "heat_load_w"}
  ]
}
