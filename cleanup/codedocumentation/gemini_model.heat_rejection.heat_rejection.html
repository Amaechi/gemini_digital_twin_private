6.1.15.1. gemini_model.heat_rejection.heat_rejection

Heat rejection to ambient: wet cooling tower or dry cooler (GM-06).

Source: RC-07 (data/knowledge/reference_cards/RC-07_heat_rejection_free_cooling_climate.md, copied in REFERENCE.md); Geothermal Direct Use Engineering & Design Guidebook (Geo-Heat Center 1989) p.262, 269; RTES data-centre cooling paper; DiPippo, Geothermal Power Plants 3e, Ch23 p.11-12; spec §7.2 GM-06.

  • Heat to reject from a chiller Q_reject = Q_cool (1 + 1/COP) (Guidebook p.262): 2.43 Q_cool at COP 0.7, 1.33 Q_cool at COP 3 (chiller_heat_rejection_w()). The chiller models already output heat_rejected_w; wire that to heat_load_w. For the absorption chiller set include_heat_rejection_auxiliaries: false: its default 2.2 % aux is the extra tower fan and pump, which this unit already counts (COOL-ABS-AUX). The electric chiller’s power_el is compressor power only (RC-06).

  • Cooling tower: leaving (cold) water T_out = T_wb + approach, approach 4-5 K (default 4.5) [A]; range ≈ 5 K [A]; fan + cooling-water pump ≈ 2 % of heat rejected [A] (Guidebook 0.077 kW/ton incremental, p.269).

  • Dry cooler: T_out = T_db + 5 K (RTES paper) [L]; fan power ≈ 3× the wet tower (DiPippo Ch23 Miravalles comparison) [L] -> fan + pump 3 × 2 % = 6 % [A, the 3× is applied to the whole 2 %]; cost ≈ 3× and area ≈ 3× (for economics, not modelled here).

  • Water use (RC-07 MAY): evaporation ≈ Q_reject / h_fg, h_fg 2.45 MJ/kg (water at ≈ 20 °C, steam tables) [A] – an upper bound (all heat by evaporation); 0 for a dry cooler.

With water_flow_rate_m3s the entering (hot) water temperature follows from the energy balance Q = ρ c_p V (T_in - T_out); without it the range is fixed and the flow is reported. All energy taken in leaves as heat: heat_to_ambient_w = heat_load_w + power_el. There is no capacity limit: size the unit at the summer design point (DB-34b). A wet tower whose fixed-approach leaving water would be at or below 0 °C is flagged in validity_warnings (freeze protection – fan control, bypass – is not modelled).
