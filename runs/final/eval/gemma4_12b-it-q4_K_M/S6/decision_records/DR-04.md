# DR-04 Cooling technology mix

- Run: `S6` · phase P5 · role system_architect
- Decision source: **llm**
- Question: Which cooling technology mix supplies 20 degC?
- **Choice: free-cooling-electric** — Free cooling + electric chillers (C2, C5)
- Decisive criterion: lowest lcoh_per_mwh + lcoc_per_mwh among guard-passed candidates without unmet demand in economics (key `lcoh_per_mwh` from economics; evidence TR-011, TR-012, TR-013, TR-014, TR-016, TR-017)
- Why: Selected based on ranking-lowest-lcoh_per_mwh among guard-passed options in economics as evidenced by TR-017. Option electric-chillers was rejected for having a higher lcoh_per_mwh. Option free-cooling-absorption was rejected because its primary candidate C4-2d was excluded by DesignGuard.
