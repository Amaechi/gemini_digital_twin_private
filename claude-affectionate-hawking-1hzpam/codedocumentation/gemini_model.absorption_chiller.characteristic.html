6.1.1.2. gemini_model.absorption_chiller.characteristic

Method B: characteristic equation (ΔΔt method) of a single-effect LiBr absorption chiller.

Source: RC-05 Method B (standard Ziegler/Hellmann characteristic-equation form; the card gives the form, the coefficients are MISSING and must be calibrated).

  • ΔΔt = (t̄_G - t̄_AC) - B (t̄_AC - t̄_E) with t̄ the arithmetic mean of inlet and outlet of the external circuits: G generator (hot water), AC absorber + condenser (cooling water in series), E evaporator (chilled water). B ≈ 1.1-1.2 (Dühring slope, LiBr) [A].

  • Capacity Q_E = s_E (ΔΔt - ΔΔt_min); drive heat Q_G = s_G (ΔΔt - ΔΔt_min,G); COP = Q_E / Q_G.

Calibration (RC-05 option ii, no datasheet found): fit s_E, ΔΔt_min, s_G, ΔΔt_min,G to Method A at its rating points (T_chw 44 °F, T_cw 85 °F, T_hw 180-240 °F). Method A gives inlet/outlet temperatures, so the mean temperatures need the external-circuit temperature changes at rating: generator ΔT 7.2 K on hot water [L Guidebook p.267], cooling-water range 5 K [A RC-07], chilled-water ΔT 6 K [A, the DB-32 design ΔT 20/26 °C; the card gives no rating value]. The fit is a weighted least-squares line through (ΔΔt, F) and (ΔΔt, F/COP) with weights 1/y, i.e. it minimises the relative error. s_E and s_G are fractions of the nominal capacity per kelvin; multiply by the nominal capacity for W/K.

Coefficients for the default B = 1.15 (DOCUMENTED_COEFFICIENTS, reproduced by calibrate_to_ghc1989() and checked in the unit tests): s_E = 0.022260 1/K, ΔΔt_min = 9.503 K, s_G = 0.032491 1/K, ΔΔt_min,G = 7.453 K (calibrated ΔΔt range 21.06-54.39 K). On the rating line the fit reproduces Method A within 1.0 % (capacity) and 2.1 % (COP).

Calibrate Method B to Method A at its rating points (RC-05 option ii).

Rating points: T_chw 44 °F, T_cw 85 °F, T_hw 180-240 °F (every 5 °F by default). Returns the coefficient dict (see fit_coefficients()); with with_report also a dict with the maximum relative capacity/COP error on the rating points and the calibrated ΔΔt range (K). Results are cached per argument set.
