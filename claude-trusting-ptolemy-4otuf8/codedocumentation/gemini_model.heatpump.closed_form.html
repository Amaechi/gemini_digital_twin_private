6.1.16.1. gemini_model.heatpump.closed_form

Closed-form heat-pump COP, robust operating point and HTHP limit screening (GM-03).

Source: RC-04 (data/knowledge/reference_cards/RC-04_heat_pump.md, copied in REFERENCE.md); Geothermal Direct Use Engineering & Design Guidebook (Geo-Heat Center 1989) Ch13 p.250, 258, Table 13.2; spec §7.2 GM-03. DEA COP-by-lift figures from RC-10 (plausibility only).

  • RC-04 eq. 1 (Carnot): COP_C = T_cond / (T_cond - T_evap) (K).

  • RC-04 eq. 2 (Lorenz): T̄ = (T_out - T_in) / ln(T_out / T_in) (log mean, K) for sink and source; COP = η_L · T̄_sink / (T̄_sink - T̄_source).

  • RC-04 eq. 3: Q_h = Q_src · COP / (COP - 1); P_el = Q_h / COP.

  • RC-04 eq. 4: refrigerant condensing / evaporating temperature = sink outlet + approach / source outlet - approach, approach 3-5 K [A] (register HP-APPROACH: 4 K).

HTHP limits at 100 °C are not covered by the 1989 book (Table 13.2: 82-104 °C, outdated): they come from a cited modern datasheet and stay MISSING until one is supplied. Without a datasheet the RC-04 default caps apply – COP ≤ 0.6 × Carnot at the refrigerant temperatures and lift (sink outlet - source outlet) ≤ 60 K [A]; they are the DesignVerifier’s register entries VER-HP-ETA and VER-HP-LIFT (check PH-14), reused here, not duplicated. The maximum sink temperature has no default: it is reported as missing and not checked.

solve_operating_point() solves the same problem as GEMINI’s Nelder-Mead path of HeatpumpBasic (sink outlet at its target, source outlet unknown; if the source outlet would fall below its minimum, it is fixed there and the sink outlet drops instead) without a starting guess: the COP is the closed-form expression above and the one unknown temperature is a bracketed scalar root (brentq) of the energy balance.

gemini_model.heatpump.closed_form.solve_operating_point(*, mode, efficiency, sink_in_degc, sink_out_target_degc, source_in_degc, sink_capacity_rate_w_k, source_capacity_rate_w_k, source_out_min_degc, carnot_cap_fraction=None, condenser_approach_k=4.0, evaporator_approach_k=4.0)[source]

Solve the heat-pump operating point with closed-form COP and a bracketed root (RC-04).

Same problem as GEMINI’s Nelder-Mead path: the sink leaves at sink_out_target_degc; the source outlet follows from Q_src = Q_h (1 - 1/COP) (RC-04 eq. 3). If it would fall below source_out_min_degc it is fixed there and the sink outlet drops instead. Capacity rates are ṁ c_p (W/K). carnot_cap_fraction (None = no cap) caps COP at that fraction of Carnot at the refrigerant temperatures (sink outlet + condenser approach, source outlet - evaporator approach).

Returns a dict: running, cop, cop_uncapped, sink_out_degc, source_out_degc, sink_heat_w, source_heat_w, power_el_w, branch (“target”, “source_floor” or “idle”) and warnings. sink_heat_w = source_heat_w + power_el_w exactly. The heat pump is idle (no exception) when a flow is zero, there is no heating need, the source is at or below its minimum, COP ≤ 1, or there is no lift (a heat exchanger suffices). Negative flows and non-finite inputs raise ValueError.
