6.1.24.1. gemini_model.thermal_storage.thermal_storage

Thermal energy storage (heat or cold) as a GEMINI DynamicModel (GM-09).

Source: RC-09 “Thermal storage” (data/knowledge/reference_cards/RC-09_district_network_ storage.md, copied in REFERENCE.md); spec §7.2 GM-09 and candidate C6 (seasonal HT-ATES).

  • State update (RC-09): E_{t+1} = E_t + η_c P_c Δt - P_d Δt / η_d - losses; here losses = λ (Δt / 1 day) E_t (standing loss, default λ = 0).

  • Recovery (energy delivered / energy charged over a cycle) = η_c η_d. Defaults put all of it on discharge (η_c = 1, η_d = recovery) so a full cycle returns exactly the literature recovery: HT-ATES 0.63 after 10 years (TU Delft; register STO-REC-HT) or cold storage 0.96 (lower end of 0.96-0.99, RTES citing Pepin 2021; register STO-REC-COLD).

  • Charge and discharge are limited by the power limits and by the stored energy / free capacity; the curtailed charge and unmet discharge are reported. Charge and discharge in the same step are refused (a dispatch error).

Daily storage adds little at daily resolution (RC-09); seasonal ATES needs an annual heat/cold balance (regulators): the cumulative charged and discharged energy are outputs for that check. The stored energy counts heat before the recovery loss: at most η_d × capacity can be delivered, so size capacity_mwh as injected energy. Outlet temperature is not modelled (an HT-ATES returns below the 100 °C supply: a booster or temperature check is needed). Units: powers in W, energies in MWh at the boundary, J internally; time step in s (default 1 day).

calculate_output(u, x=None)[source]

Calculate the step: actual charge/discharge power, end-of-step energy and losses.

Inputs u: charge_power_w or discharge_power_w (requested, W, ≥ 0; not both), optional time_step_s. x is the state from initialize_state() / update_state() (None → initial state). power_th is the heat (or cold) delivered by discharging; charging draws charge_power_w from the plant. Do not add power_th to the plant’s production totals (the stored heat was produced upstream and would be counted twice): use net_heat_to_plant_w = discharge - charge.
