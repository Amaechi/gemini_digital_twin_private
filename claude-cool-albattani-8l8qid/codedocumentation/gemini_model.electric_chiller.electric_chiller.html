6.1.9.1. gemini_model.electric_chiller.electric_chiller

Electric (vapour-compression) chiller with a Carnot-efficiency COP (GM-05).

Source: RC-06 (data/knowledge/reference_cards/RC-06_electric_chiller.md, copied in REFERENCE.md); Geothermal Direct Use Engineering & Design Guidebook (Geo-Heat Center 1989) p.257-258, 262, 266; spec §3.3 and §7.2 GM-05.

  • COP = η T_evap / (T_cond - T_evap) (K) on the refrigerant basis (T_evap and T_cond below include the -4 K / +3 K offsets). RC-06 “Temperature basis of η”: the Guidebook’s 0.39-0.40 (COP 4.39 at 45 °F chilled / 90 °F condenser leaving water, p.257-258) is on the water basis; on the model’s basis the same point gives η = 4.39 × 32 / 276.35 = 0.508 [D]. With carnot_efficiency: None (default) η is calibrated by calibrated_carnot_efficiency() from the model’s own approach parameters, so the Guidebook point is always reproduced (COOL-ELC); an explicit value overrides it (keep it on the refrigerant basis – do not mix bases).

  • T_evap = T_chilled,supply - 4 K [A] (20 °C supply -> 16 °C).

  • T_cond = T_cw,leaving + 3 K with a cooling tower, T_cw,leaving = T_wb + approach (4-5 K, default 4.5) + range (~5 K) [A]; or T_cond = T_db + 8 K with a dry cooler [A]. If cooling_water_temperature_in_degc (water from a tower or dry cooler) is given, T_cond = T_cw,in + range + 3 K (water-cooled condenser) for either option. The dry_cooler option is RC-06’s air-cooled condensing (T_db + 8 K); a water-loop dry cooler (heat_rejection dry cooler, T_db + 5 K) feeding the condenser gives T_db + 13 K (COP 5.26 vs 6.80 at 25 °C) – a warning says which formula was used.

  • COP capped at 8 and floored at 3 [A]; P_el = Q_cool / COP is compressor power only (tower fan and condenser-water pump belong to a heat_rejection component, RC-06); Q_reject = Q_cool + P_el. With η 0.508 the cap of 8 binds at the Dutch design points.

Condenser sensitivity: the model gives d ln COP / dT_cond = -1/(T_cond - T_evap) (output condenser_sensitivity_per_k), -3.1 %/K at the Guidebook point (lift 32 K on the refrigerant basis), steeper than the Guidebook data (≈ -2.6 %/K of condenser-water temperature, p.257-258): the model’s COP falls faster in hot weather (documented check).

Capacity is the nominal capacity (no temperature derating); unmet load is reported. At 20 °C chilled supply and Dutch summer conditions RC-06 expects COP ≈ 6-8; with the calibrated η the model reaches the cap (8) there and drops below it only in hotter weather (e.g. 6.7 with a dry cooler at 30 °C dry-bulb).
