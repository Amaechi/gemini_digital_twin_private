6.1.1.3. gemini_model.absorption_chiller.ghc1989

Method A: Geo-Heat Center 1989 absorption-chiller correlation (temperatures in °F).

Source: RC-05 Method A – Geothermal Direct Use Engineering & Design Guidebook (Geo-Heat Center, 1989), Ch14 p.261-269; the correlations were decoded from the TI-59 program listing on p.268 (RC-05 “Verification status”).

  • Hot-water temperature factor F_T = (-239 + 1.65 T_hw - 0.001 T_hw^2) / 100.

  • Condition factor F_c = (323 - 0.838 T_chw - (3.77 - 0.0358 T_chw) T_cw) / 100, T_chw leaving chilled water (valid 40-50 °F), T_cw entering cooling water (valid 75-90 °F).

  • Capacity fraction F = F_T F_c (fraction of the nominal rating; nominal = Q_required / F).

  • COP = 0.67 (55.4 + 0.185 T_hw) / 100 (depends on T_hw only).

Rating basis (p.261-262): 12 psi steam / ~118 °C hot water, 85 °F (29.4 °C) cooling water, 44 °F (6.7 °C) chilled water, COP 0.65-0.70. Outside its validity (e.g. our 20 °C chilled water) the correlation is a conservative lower bound only (RC-05).
